Overview

Designed and developed a complete WordPress website for a restaurant group, including online booking functionality, responsive design across all devices, menu presentation and a content management system the client could update independently.

The Challenge

The client needed a website that looked premium, loaded quickly, worked on mobile devices and included a booking system that integrated with their existing reservation management. The site also needed to be easy for non-technical staff to update menus, images and information.

My Role

I handled the complete project: visual design, WordPress setup, Elementor page building, responsive development, booking plugin configuration, content structure, performance optimisation and client training for content management.

Outcome

The website launched successfully with positive feedback from the client. The booking system reduced phone enquiries, the responsive design improved mobile user experience, and the CMS allowed the client to update content independently without developer support.
