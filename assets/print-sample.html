My Role

Artwork Preparation, Print Production, Large Format Operation, Quality Control

Overview

During my time at Lime Inc., I gained hands-on experience in commercial print production — learning how designs translate from screen to physical material, understanding material properties, operating large-format equipment and performing quality control on finished products.

What I Learned

This role bridged the gap between digital design and physical production. I learned that a design that looks perfect on screen can fail in production if the artwork isn't properly prepared, if the material isn't suitable, or if colour management isn't handled correctly. This practical understanding now informs every design I create.

Key Responsibilities

  • Preparing artwork files for large-format and commercial print
  • Operating large-format printing equipment
  • Producing signage, banners, promotional materials and branded items
  • Checking colour accuracy, registration and material quality
  • Understanding bleed, safe areas, resolution and production specifications
