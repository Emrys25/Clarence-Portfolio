Overview

At Lilly Loompa, I worked with discarded and reclaimed materials to design and manufacture functional and decorative products. This experience developed my understanding of sustainable production, creative problem-solving, material properties and physical fabrication processes.

The Process

Each product began with identifying suitable materials — understanding what they were, what condition they were in and what they could realistically become. I then developed concepts, prepared materials through cutting, cleaning and treatment, manufactured the products through assembly and construction, applied finishing processes and performed quality checks on every completed piece.

What This Demonstrates

  • Ability to take an idea from concept to finished physical product
  • Creative problem-solving when working with unpredictable materials
  • Practical manufacturing skills and attention to craft
  • Understanding of sustainability and material recovery
  • Initiative and resourcefulness in a production environment
