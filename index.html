About Me

Creative professional combining design, digital and production skills

I'm Clarence Ruwizhi, a multidisciplinary creative based in Stellenbosch, Western Cape. My experience spans graphic design, branding, web development, digital content, large-format print, photography and video, developed through practical work in commercial, hospitality and independent creative environments.

I'm self-taught in design and digital production, and I believe that practical experience is the strongest foundation for creative work. Whether I'm designing a brand identity, building a WordPress website, preparing artwork for large-format print or developing promotional campaigns, I approach every project with the same commitment to quality and attention to detail.
