about

The person behind the code

Developer, creative technologist and founder of Honvria Studio. Based in Stellenbosch, Cape Town.

My name is Clarence Ruwizhi. I am a self-taught full-stack developer and multidisciplinary creative working across web development, brand identity, graphic design, photography, video and print production.

I started my creative career in graphic design and print production, working hands-on with prepress, colour separation and large-format output at LimeInc.Tech. From there I moved into brand identity and campaign design, creating visual systems for events like the CoffeeMM Barista Battle and Barista Academy.

But I realised that designing something was only half the equation. I wanted to build the things I designed. So I taught myself to code.

Today my primary focus is web and application development. I am currently building a full-stack application using Node.js, TypeScript, Python and Tailwind CSS. I also build custom WordPress themes from scratch, without page builders or plugin stacks, because I believe hand-coded solutions are cleaner, faster and more maintainable.

In January 2026 I founded Honvria Studio, an independent creative studio that brings together development, design and visual storytelling under one roof. The studio is both a client-facing practice and the creative framework through which I approach all my work.

philosophy

Why I build from scratch

I do not use Elementor. I do not use Divi. I do not stack twelve plugins together and hope the site holds up. Every website I build is coded by hand, tested across devices and optimised for performance from the first line.

This is not about being difficult. It is about control. When you build something yourself, you understand every part of it. You know why a section loads slowly, you know where the accessibility gap is, and you know exactly what to change when the brief evolves.

The same philosophy applies to my design work. I would rather spend the time creating a considered, intentional solution than assemble something quickly from templates. The result is work that is cleaner, more distinctive and built to last.
